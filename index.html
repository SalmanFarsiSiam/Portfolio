Frame 002 / Who is behind the cut

Freelance · Remote · Fast turnaround

He started in Photoshop four years ago, fixing images for friends and small pages, and kept going until the retouching stopped looking like retouching. Design came out of the same habit — posters, logos and social sets built on structure instead of trends.

Video came later and took over. Two years of cutting reels, YouTube pieces and promos taught him the thing that matters most: pacing. A good edit is felt before it is noticed.

He works directly with the person who needs the work, keeps revisions honest, and prefers a clear brief over a long call.
